外部文件 (house.fbx)
    ↓
ModelManager::CreateFromFile("house.fbx")
    ↓
Importer::ReadFile → aiScene
    ├── aiMesh[] (几何数据)
    └── aiMaterial[] (材质数据)
    ↓
Model::CreateFromFile
    ├── 遍历 aiMesh → 创建 MeshData
    │   ├── device->CreateBuffer → m_pVertices (ID3D11Buffer)
    │   ├── device->CreateBuffer → m_pNormals (ID3D11Buffer)
    │   ├── device->CreateBuffer → m_pTexcoordArrays (ID3D11Buffer[])
    │   ├── device->CreateBuffer → m_pIndices (ID3D11Buffer)
    │   └── m_MaterialIndex = pAiMesh->mMaterialIndex
    │
    └── 遍历 aiMaterial → 创建 Material
        ├── material.Set("$AmbientColor", vec)
        ├── material.Set("$DiffuseColor", vec)
        ├── material.Set("$Opacity", value)
        └── pAiMaterial->GetTexture → TextureManager::CreateFromFile
            └── 创建 ID3D11ShaderResourceView → 存储到 TextureManager
    ↓
Model (存储在 ModelManager::m_Models)
    ├── vector<MeshData> (GPU 几何)
    └── vector<Material> (材质属性)
    ↓
GameObject::SetModel(Model*)
    ↓
渲染循环：GameObject::Draw(BasicEffect)
    ├── 遍历 MeshData
    ├── SetMaterial(materials[m_MaterialIndex])
    │   ├── 提取属性 → 写入常量缓冲区
    │   └── 从 TextureManager 取 SRV → 绑定到管线
    ├── SetWorldMatrix(transform)
    ├── Apply
    │   ├── 设置矩阵到常量缓冲区
    │   └── EffectPass::Apply
    │       ├── VSSetShader
    │       ├── PSSetShader
    │       ├── VSSetConstantBuffers
    │       ├── PSSetShaderResources
    │       └── PSSetSamplers
    ├── IASetVertexBuffers
    ├── IASetIndexBuffer
    └── DrawIndexed
    ↓
GPU 渲染到屏幕
